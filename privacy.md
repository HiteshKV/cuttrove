# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 26 October 2026

cuttrove ("the app", "we") is a publishing tool run by an independent developer. It posts short video clips to TikTok and Instagram accounts whose owners have connected and authorised it. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

## What we collect

When you connect a TikTok or Instagram account, the platform shares the following with the app:

- **Basic profile information:** account ID, username, display name and profile picture.
- **Access tokens:** these let the app publish posts that you have chosen to share.

The app also stores the videos and captions you choose to publish, and the publish status the platform returns (for example, whether a post succeeded).

We do **not** collect your password, messages, followers list, contacts or any other content from your account.

## How we use it

We use your data only to:

- publish the videos and captions you choose to your own connected account; and
- show you whether each post succeeded.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

## Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your data with anyone. Data goes only to the platform you are posting to (TikTok or Instagram), and only what is needed to publish your post.

## Storage and security

Access tokens and account information are stored on a private server controlled by the app's operator. They are never published and are used only by the app to publish your posts.

## Retention and deletion

We keep your data only while your account is connected. You can disconnect at any time:

- **TikTok:** Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions → remove cuttrove.
- **Instagram:** Settings → Apps and websites → remove cuttrove.

To have all stored data and tokens deleted, email the address below. We delete everything within 30 days of your request.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
